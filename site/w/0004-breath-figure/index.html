Breath Figure

A night-bus window. Glass remembers every body that touched it, and only breath shows it. Wiping moves the grease and never removes it, so each attempt to clean the pane signs itself on the next breath. At the last stop the heater clears the fog but not the marks.

Not tested: no stranger has read the finished, shaded version cold, so the dark track a wipe leaves may look like writing. The fog grain was only seen in a software renderer, the keyboard hold was read in code and never run, and touch screens were not tried on hardware.

The look of the fog and the marks is drawn from everyday experience of breathing on glass. We have not checked it against a primary source on breath figures.

How to interact

  • Rest a finger on the glass and hold: a haze answers, then breath fogs the pane. Hold in a few places; the glass keeps marks you did not make.
  • Keyboard: focus the window (Tab), then hold Space or Enter to breathe at the centre of the pane.
  • Drag to write through the fog. The fog fades, and your line returns with the next breath.
  • The ride has cold stretches, where fog comes fast, and warm stops, where it hardly takes. Wait for the last stop.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a control, Enter to open, Escape to close. If you prefer reduced motion, the fog moves slowly and calmly.
