Plain Hunt

A bell you let go of strikes about two seconds later. You commit before you hear. Five other bells ring a fixed plain hunt around you. If your blow lands late, the bell behind you in that row bends to cover, and the row is true again within a few bells. The band is a rule, not people: its bells never err. The only mistakes are yours, and the record draws every one of them at its true time.

How to interact

  • Press and hold to take the rope as the bell rises. Release to ring. Space or Enter does the same.
  • Hold too long and the bell creeps past balance and is set: it goes quiet and the band stands. Press once to ring again.
  • The bell's edge lightens while you hold inside the slanted patch of daylight. Hold past it and the edge stays dark.
  • Sound is optional. Nothing is needed that sound alone carries, and it starts only after your first press.
  • With reduced motion on, the bell is drawn still and an arc with one dot shows its angle. Timing and rules are the same.
