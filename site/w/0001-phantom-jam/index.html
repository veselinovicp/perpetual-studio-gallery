Phantom Jam

A slowdown can outlive every car that is in it. Brake once and it walks backward through the drivers behind you, all the way round the ring, until it comes back to you. Ease off early and gently enough and it dissolves into the gap. Brake too hard and you start a new one. This is a small simulation of cars on a circle, not a model of any real road.

How to interact

  • Press and hold anywhere, or hold Space, to ease off. Let go to drive on.
  • A tap only flashes your lights; it takes a few seconds of holding to start a wave.
  • Watch the mirror, then the road ahead.
